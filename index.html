Selected work

The kinds of problems I'm brought in to solve. Details are kept general out of respect for the organizations involved.

Platform delivery

A modern content experience across a regulated enterprise

The challenge

Give every user a modern way to work with controlled documents without disrupting the records processes the business depends on.

My approach

Led the program from business case and governance through architecture, phased rollout and change management, working closely with the vendor and records leadership.

The result

A new interface live across the organization, validated in every environment and handed over cleanly to operations.

Reliability

Turning an unstable platform into a dependable one

The challenge

Recurring outages and an aging runtime were putting availability and user confidence at risk.

My approach

Partnered with the vendor on targeted redesign: high availability for authentication, server tuning, product fixes and a runtime modernization.

The result

A more resilient, supportable foundation and lower operational risk for day-to-day service.

Integration

Untangling a long-running cross-system integration issue

The challenge

A business-critical integration between core enterprise systems had been unreliable for years, threatening data integrity.

My approach

Brought infrastructure, application and vendor teams to one table, then coordinated testing, deployments and code fixes end to end.

The result

A more reliable integration and stronger protection for the records that depend on it.

Strategy & AI

Getting a content platform ready for cloud and AI

The challenge

New AI initiatives wanted access to platform content, and the platform itself needed a clear long-term direction.

My approach

Led an AI proof of concept, set guardrails for data access and system load, and shaped a multi-year roadmap covering cloud deployment and AI enablement.

The result

AI teams got the content they needed without putting stability at risk, and leadership gained a sound basis for investment decisions.
